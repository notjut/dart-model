What changes the result?

Experimental sandbox, separate from official picks. Scoring uses current opponent-adjusted offense and defense where available. Home advantage is a user scenario of 3 margin points. Weather changes the total by the amount shown; these effects are assumptions, not fitted injury or weather models. Kickoff and stadium labels customize the replay, but add no untested numerical bonus. No current lineup or injury adjustment. More runs stabilize the estimate; they do not validate it.
